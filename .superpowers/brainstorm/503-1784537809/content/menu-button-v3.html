Theme picker — always 3, ordered by rule

The dropdown’s theme list is capped at three, with a “Builder →” to reach the rest and manage/create.

Ordering rule:
• No custom themes → the 3 most-recently-used.
• Any custom themes → your themes first, newest-created first, then recently-used fill the remaining slots (to 3).
• Shown below = you just created Newsprint: Newsprint → last used (Default) → the one before (Sepia).
